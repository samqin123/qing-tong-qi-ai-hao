Abstract In academic circle the experts consider popularly that the style of Wangziwu Ding with girdling waist and bulging belly has a close relationship with the aesthetic test of Ling king of the Chu state enjoying slender waist. Form researching the inscription on this Ding we can conclude the time of making it. According to the documents the newest archaological materials and the development law of decorative arts we can confirm that the style of Wangziwu Ding tri - pod is influenced by the aesthetic test of Zhu king of the Chu state.
